## A — geometry

- Shows: ~120 small freckle-like dots across the cheeks and nose
- Arrangement: two loose clusters under the eyes, a few across the bridge of the nose
- Impression: playful, sun-weathered, a bit mischievous
- Status: explored on branch `A`. Built as planned: 120 freckles in two cheek clusters and a band across the nose.

## B — assets

- Image 1: a knitted wool hat, sitting on top of the head (I'll draw it myself)
- Image 2: round glasses, placed over the eyes (free-licensed image from OpenGameArt)
- Impression: cozy, bookish
- Status: explored on branch `B`. Instead of drawing the hat, I used one from the "Hats n Hats" pack on itch.io, and the glasses came from an itch.io pack rather than OpenGameArt. Both are pixel art, so the portrait reads more like retro game than cozy.

## C — color and curves

- Gradient: shades the head from warm orange at the top to cool blue at the chin
- Bézier curve: a lopsided, uncertain smile
- Impression: a nervous character caught mid-thought
- Status: explored on branch `C`. Built as planned: orange to blue head gradient and a lopsided smile.

## Criterion for choosing

Whichever one reads clearly as a distinct personality at small size.

## Decision

Chose C. Against the criterion, it reads the most clear as a distinct personality at small size. The orange to blue head and lopsided smile still show a nervous face even when its tiny. A's freckles blur and B's glasses and hat were also a bit blurry when small. C was rebased onto main after the buildFace() refactor, so its gradient and smile now live inside buildFace(). A and B remain on their own branches as a record of the alternatives I explored.
